<b>NarOffice</b> is a simple, fast and free office suite. View and edit Word, Excel and PowerPoint files on your phone, without an account, ads, tracking or internet access.

It reads Office Open XML files directly instead of bundling a desktop office suite, so it starts quickly and stays small. Only what you change is rewritten: everything else in your file is saved exactly as it was.

<p><b>Documents (.docx)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Mobile-friendly reading view with pinch to zoom</li>
<li>Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, superscript, subscript, font size, text colour and highlight</li>
<li>Titles and headings, alignment, bulleted and numbered lists, indents</li>
<li>Tables, pictures and page breaks</li>
<li>Find and replace, undo and redo</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Spreadsheets (.xlsx, .csv)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Fast grid with frozen panes, merged cells, colours, borders and number formats</li>
<li>Formulas with more than 60 functions, including SUM, IF, VLOOKUP, SUMIF, INDEX and MATCH</li>
<li>Insert and delete rows and columns, several sheets, copy and paste, AutoSum</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Presentations (.pptx)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Slides shown with their themes, layouts, shapes, pictures and tables</li>
<li>Edit text, move and resize objects, add text boxes and pictures</li>
<li>Add, duplicate, reorder and delete slides, full-screen slideshow</li>
</ul>

Old binary formats (.doc, .xls, .ppt) are not supported.
